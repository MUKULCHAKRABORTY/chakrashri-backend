Our Story

Twelve Years Of Bringing The Sacred Home

Chakrashri began in 2014 as a single stall near a Nashik temple, verifying sphatik pieces for pilgrims who'd been sold glass. Today we work with artisan clusters across India and a network of verified priests and astrologers, but the original promise hasn't changed: nothing leaves our warehouse until it's been checked by hand.

Authenticity First

Every sphatik, rudraksha and metal idol is checked against material standards before listing.

Made With Care

We work directly with artisan families in Jaipur, Moradabad and Nashik.

Verified Priests

Every priest and astrologer on our platform is interviewed and background-checked.

Easy To Trust

Transparent pricing, real reviews, and a 7-day return window on everything we ship.

Home/Policies
Shipping Returns & Refunds Privacy Terms Of Service

Shipping Policy

We dispatch every order within 24–48 hours of confirmation. Shipping is free across India on orders above ₹999; a flat ₹79 fee applies to smaller orders. Delivery typically takes 2–7 business days depending on your location.

  • Fragile items (idols, sphatik pieces) are double-boxed with cushioning.
  • You'll receive tracking details by SMS and email once your order ships.
  • Puja samagri kits are packed fresh and dispatched closer to your requested date where relevant.

Return & Refund Policy

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Please record an unboxing video — it speeds up claims for any transit damage considerably.

  • Energized items, engraved yantras, and custom puja kits are final sale once dispatched.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval.
  • Puja and astrology bookings can be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to fulfil your order or booking — your name, contact details, shipping address, and for astrology bookings, birth details you choose to share. This information is never sold to third parties.

  • Payment details are processed by our payment partner and are not stored on our servers.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by writing to support@chakrashri.com.

Terms Of Service

By using this site you agree to provide accurate information for orders and bookings, and to use our AI assistant and booking tools for their intended purpose. Prices are listed in Indian Rupees and are subject to change without notice; the price at checkout is the price charged.

  • Puja and astrology services are performed by independent, verified professionals; outcomes and interpretations are for guidance and reflect traditional practice, not a guarantee of results.
  • Chakrashri reserves the right to cancel orders in cases of pricing errors or stock unavailability, with a full refund issued.
